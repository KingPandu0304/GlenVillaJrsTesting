Who We Are

More Than Just
A Football Club

Since 1955, Glen Villa Juniors FC has been the heartbeat of grassroots youth football in Great Glen, Leicestershire. Our junior football club welcomes players of all abilities — from first kicks to competitive league football — with kids' football teams spanning U7 all the way through to U17.

Our qualified coaches are passionate about player development, teamwork, and creating a positive, inclusive environment where every young player can thrive — on and off the pitch. Based at Great Glen Recreational, we're proud to be the local youth football club for families across Great Glen and the wider Leicestershire area.
