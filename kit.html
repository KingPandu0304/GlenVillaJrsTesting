What's Included

The full kit includes a match shirt, shorts, and socks in club colours. Training wear is also available separately through the shop.

Goalkeeper kits are available in a separate colourway — ask your coach for details.

Personalisation

Players may be allowed to choose a squad number for their shirt at the time of ordering. Speak to your team coach for your squad number before ordering.

How to Order

01

Check Sizing

Use the sizing guide below to find the right fit for your child before placing your order.

02

Talk to your coach

Discuss sizing options with your team coach before the team places your order.

03

Add Personalisation

Optionally add a squad number — check with your coach for the correct number first and if they are being chosen for you.

04

Collect your kit

At one of the first training sessionsof the season, all kit will be available for collection.
